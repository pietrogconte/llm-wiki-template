# Hydration 70% vs 75% (fictional example note)

Same day, bread flour, 20% levain, 2% salt, kitchen at 24 °C (thermometer).

- 75%: sticky, 4 h 30 bulk, open crumb, the dough spread sideways.
Baked in the cast iron pot: 20 min lid on at 250 °C + 20 min lid off at 230 °C.
Sticking with 70% for now.
