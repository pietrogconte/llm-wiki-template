# Cast iron pot

> **Fictional example content** from `llm-wiki-template`.

A lidded pot (*dutch oven*) that traps the dough's own steam early in the bake.

## Use

- 20 min lid on at 250 °C + 20 min lid off at 230 °C.
- Preheat it together with the oven.
