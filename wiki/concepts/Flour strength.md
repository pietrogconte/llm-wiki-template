# Flour strength

> **Fictional example content** from `llm-wiki-template`.

The flour's ability to form strong gluten, measured by **W** (alveograph) and the **P/L** ratio.

## Points

- W 260–300: 24 h pizza ([[Type 00 flour]]).
- W 380: panettone, which ferments long and carries fat and sugar.
- Weak flour in a long fermentation: the dough loses structure.
