# Maillard reaction

> **Fictional example content** from `llm-wiki-template`.

Reaction between sugars and amino acids above ~140 °C that browns the crust and creates aroma.

## Points

- 250 °C gave a darker, more aromatic crust than 230 °C.
- Sugar-rich doughs (panettone) brown fast: lower oven or a cover.
- Depends on the real temperature of the [[Home oven]].
