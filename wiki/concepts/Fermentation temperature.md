# Fermentation temperature

> **Fictional example content** from `llm-wiki-template`.

Dough and room temperature control how fast fermentation goes.

## Records

| Recipe | Condition | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Bread 70% | 24 °C | 5 h bulk |
| Pizza | ~22 °C | 24 h |
| Focaccia | 2 h at 24 °C + ~4 °C | 36 h |
| Panettone | 26 °C | 12 h (1st impasto) |

## Points

- Cold slows fermentation and builds flavor; heat speeds it up.
- Tied to [[Natural fermentation]]: a slow levain in a cold room delays everything.
