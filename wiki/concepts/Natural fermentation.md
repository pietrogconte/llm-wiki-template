# Natural fermentation

> **Fictional example content** from `llm-wiki-template`.

Fermentation by wild yeasts and lactic acid bacteria from a **levain**, instead of commercial yeast.

## Points

- Ready levain: doubles within a few hours and passes the float test.
- Rich doughs (panettone) need a very active levain: several refreshments in a row.
- The pace depends on the [[Fermentation temperature]].
