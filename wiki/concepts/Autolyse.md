# Autolyse

> **Fictional example content** from `llm-wiki-template`.

Resting flour with water, before adding levain and salt, so gluten starts forming on its own.

## Points

- 30 min made the dough more extensible; 60 min brought no extra gain.
- Most useful for high-[[Hydration]] doughs.
