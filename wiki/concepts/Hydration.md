# Hydration

> **Fictional example content** from `llm-wiki-template`.

Water relative to flour (flour = 100%). 700 g of water per 1 kg of flour = **70%**.

## Recorded ranges

| Recipe | Hydration |
|---|---|
| Neapolitan pizza | 62% |
| Bread | 70% (75% being tested) |
| Focaccia | 80% |

## Points

- More water opens the crumb but demands shaping technique and folds.
- [[Autolyse]] helps very wet doughs build structure.
